PRACTICAL MOVEMENT

Commuter Tips for Abuja

Confirm Exact Boarding Points

Major junctions can have separate lanes for short-hop drops and longer express stretches—always confirm the final stop before boarding.

Add Buffer During Rush Hours

School runs, market days, and rainy mornings can double transit times along expressways and main arterial junctions.

Keep Small Denominations Ready

Having smaller naira notes on hand speeds up fare settlement at busy bus stops and avoids delays.
